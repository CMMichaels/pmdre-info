PMDRE — Private Research Application

About the Application

PMDRE is a privately developed, single-user, noncommercial research application designed to study U.S. equity market data, momentum patterns, and potential trading opportunities.

Market Data Access

The application is intended to use authorized market-data access for research and analysis. It does not provide brokerage account management or automated order execution.

Trading and Execution

PMDRE does not transmit brokerage buy or sell orders. Any real-money trading decisions and order submissions are performed independently by the individual user through a separate brokerage interface.

Availability

This is a private research project. The software is not offered for public use, subscription, redistribution, or commercial trading services.
