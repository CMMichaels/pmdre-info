Privacy Policy

Python Momentum Discovery Research Engine (PMDRE)

Effective date: November 5, 2026

1. Application Purpose

PMDRE is a privately operated, single-user, noncommercial application for researching U.S. equity market data and momentum behavior. It is not offered as a public service.

2. Information Processing

The application is designed to process market data and research observations for the individual operator. Research records may be stored locally for analysis and evaluation.

3. OAuth Authorization

PMDRE is intended to request Alpaca OAuth authorization limited to market-data access using the data scope. It does not request trading or account-write authorization.

4. Credentials and Security

Application credentials and access tokens are intended to be handled outside the public website and are not published in this repository. The informational website does not provide brokerage account access.

5. Information Sharing

PMDRE is not intended to sell personal information or redistribute licensed market data. Market-data requests are subject to the applicable provider's services and policies.

6. Third-Party Services

Authorized market-data access may involve Alpaca. This informational website is hosted separately through GitHub Pages. These services maintain their own privacy and security policies.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated if the application's capabilities or data-handling practices change.
